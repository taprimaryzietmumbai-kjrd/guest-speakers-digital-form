Coordinator workflow

  1. Enter the workshop title and target group.
  2. Enter the speaker's name, WhatsApp number, email, session title, agreed date and time slot.
  3. Generate the personal link to send the invitation email automatically; use WhatsApp to share the link as well if needed.
  4. Repeat for each speaker so each receives a link with their own name.

Important: An email address is required for automatic invitations. Submission notifications are sent to the configured ZIET Mumbai coordinator addresses.
